## Decisions / D-drafts
- **D-draft (U3):** the Repositories "last scan failed" filter matches a last scan of status **failed OR partial**
  (a partial scan means ≥1 scanner failed → belongs in a "problem scans" filter). Reversible, UI-only, no domain/
  security/API-contract impact.
- D-084/OD-26 (already accepted) governs the source-link builder; `ui/src/lib/sourceLink.ts` is its implementation,
  guarded by `sourceLink.test.ts`. The warning-code → coverage-banner map (approval item 10) is in
  `docs/reports/M8.6-worklog.md` and unit-tested in `coverage.test.ts`.
